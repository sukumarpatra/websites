🔍 The 5 things costing you bookings
🌐No websiteHigh impact

The hotel has no website of its own

When a guest searches “NSR Grand Luxury Rooms Hyderabad”, they don't find you — they find OYO, Expedia and Booking.com pages about you. You have a 4.8 Google rating and a 4.9 on OYO with 44 reviews, but there is nowhere a guest can land that is actually yours. Every link a guest clicks belongs to a third party.
The fix
A simple one-page direct-booking site: your rooms, photos, location, and a WhatsApp booking button. Built in a day, hosted on your own domain.
💡 Guests find the hotel directly — and Google has somewhere to send the searchers you're losing today.
💸OTA commissionsHigh impact

Every booking flows through OYO and OTAs — and they take a cut

Your OYO page shows rooms at ₹1,202/night (with “80% off” discounts). OTA commission on such bookings typically runs 15–25%. A guest who books direct through your own site or WhatsApp is revenue that stays fully with the hotel — same room, same rate, no middleman.
The fix
Put a direct-booking button (WhatsApp or a simple form) on your own site and on your Google Business Profile, and gently encourage repeat guests to book direct.
💡 Every direct booking saves the commission — and builds your own guest database.
📱WhatsApp bookingHigh impact

No WhatsApp booking — travellers in India book over chat

For budget and mid-range hotels, most enquiries start on WhatsApp: room photos, rates, check-in time, “can you hold a room?”. Right now a traveller has to call during office hours or dig through OYO. The hotels in your area that answer WhatsApp instantly win those bookings.
The fix
A WhatsApp booking line with pre-filled messages and instant replies — including an AI receptionist that can answer room questions 24/7 even when the front desk is busy.
💡 Enquiries answered in seconds, day or night — the difference between booked and lost.
🗺️Google presenceMedium impact

Your Google listing has no website to point to

Your Google Business Profile shows a phone number but no website link — so guests arriving from Maps hit a dead end. Meanwhile “hotels near SR Nagar” searches show competitors who do have sites.
The fix
Add your new site to the Google listing, fix the hotel name everywhere, and complete the profile (photos, categories, description).
💡 Your 4.8★ rating starts working for you in local search instead of only on Maps.
🏷️Brand confusionMedium impact

Your name is different on every platform

Google Maps says “NSR Grand Luxury Rooms”, OYO says “Hotel O NSR Grandhotel”, Expedia says “Hotel O NSR Grandhotel”. A guest who likes you on OYO can't find the same name on Google — and vice versa. Inconsistent names split reviews and trust.
The fix
Pick one official name (e.g. NSR Grand Luxury Rooms), use it consistently on Google, OYO, Expedia and your new site.
💡 Reviews and reputation accumulate under one name — stronger everywhere.
