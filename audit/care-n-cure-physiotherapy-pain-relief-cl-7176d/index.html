🔍 The 4 things costing you patients
⚠️Duplicate versionsHigh impact

Your website is live on 4 different addresses at once

Right now all four of these open your homepage: http://carencurephysiotherapyrehabs.com, https://carencurephysiotherapyrehabs.com, the same two with www. — and none of them redirect to one main address. Google treats them as four separate websites, so the ranking power you've built gets divided by four. Anyone who lands on the http:// version also sees a “Not secure” warning before your page even loads.
The fix
One permanent redirect (301) so every address points to a single https:// version, plus a canonical tag that tells Google which one is official. A 15-minute server-level fix.
💡 All your SEO strength consolidates on one address — better rankings for “physiotherapy in Nallakunta”.
📱WhatsApp sharingHigh impact

When a patient shares your site on WhatsApp, it shows a bare link

Your pages have no Open Graph tags, so WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram can't build a preview. When someone forwards your website to a friend — which is exactly how physiotherapy clinics get new patients — they see a plain grey URL instead of your clinic name, photo and a “Book now” line.
The fix
Add Open Graph + Twitter card tags to every page: clinic name, tagline, location, and a photo. Done once in the site template.
💡 Every shared link becomes a free mini-advertisement for the clinic, with your brand on it.
📊AnalyticsMedium impact

No analytics — you can't see where bookings actually come from

No analytics code was detected on the site. You don't know how many people come from Google Maps, from WhatsApp shares, or from search — so you can't tell which effort is paying off.
The fix
Install a privacy-respecting analytics tag (one line of code) with a simple monthly report.
💡 Know exactly which channel brings patients, and spend your marketing money there.
🔗Social presenceLow impact

No links to your social profiles

The site doesn't link to Facebook, Instagram or YouTube. For a clinic with “Trusted since 2001”, patient stories and recovery videos build enormous trust — and new patients actively look for them.
The fix
Add social links to the header/footer and start posting recovery stories weekly (we can help).
💡 Patients trust a clinic they can see. Social proof shortens the decision to book.
