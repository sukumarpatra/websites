🔍 The 4 things costing you patients
📊AnalyticsHigh impact

There is no analytics on the site — so nobody knows where patients come from

I checked the page source: there is no Google Analytics, no Google Tag Manager and no pixel anywhere. Right now you cannot tell whether patients find you through Google, Instagram, WhatsApp forwards or word of mouth — which means every marketing decision (and every rupee spent) is a guess.
The fix
Install a free analytics tag, plus one simple conversion event: a tap on the call button or the WhatsApp button. Ten minutes of setup, and from then on you see exactly which channel brings appointments.
💡 You stop guessing: you see which channel brings the most patients, and pour effort into that one.
📩After-hoursHigh impact

A patient who visits at 10pm has no way to leave their details

The site has click-to-call and WhatsApp — both excellent. But both need the patient to act right now: call, or type a message. There is no simple form where someone can leave their name, phone and problem in 30 seconds. Patients researching physiotherapy at night often close the tab and come back tomorrow — to a competitor's form.
The fix
Add a short enquiry form (name, phone, what's hurting) that lands in your inbox and WhatsApp instantly — three fields, nothing more.
💡 Enquiries captured 24/7, even when the clinic is closed.
💬WhatsAppMedium impact

WhatsApp works — but only when someone is free to answer

The WhatsApp button is in place and patients do use it. But between appointments, during treatment and after hours, messages sit unanswered. For a physio clinic the first question is almost always the same — \u201cdo you treat my problem, and when can I come?\u201d — and a slow reply usually means the patient has already messaged the next clinic.
The fix
An AI receptionist on your WhatsApp number answers the standard questions instantly (services, timings, location, home visits), captures the patient's problem and preferred time, and hands over to your staff for anything clinical. It never gives medical advice.
💡 Every enquiry gets an instant, accurate reply — including at 11pm — without hiring anyone.
🖼️ImagesLow impact

6 of 20 images have no alt text

Alt text is how Google \u201csees\u201d images — and how image search sends you visitors. Six images on the homepage are invisible to it.
The fix
Add a short descriptive alt text to each image (e.g. \u201cphysiotherapy session at Legend Physiotherapy, Kothapet\u201d).
💡 Extra visits from Google Images, plus better accessibility for patients using screen readers.
