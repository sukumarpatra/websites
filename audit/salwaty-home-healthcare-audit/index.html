🔍 The 5 things costing you enquiries
📱WhatsApp sharingHigh impact

When a family shares your site on WhatsApp, it shows a bare grey link

I checked every page: there are no Open Graph or Twitter card tags. So when a happy family forwards salwaty.com to a friend — the single most common way home-care recommendations travel in Dubai — the message shows a plain grey URL with no name, no photo, no star rating. Your 281 reviews never travel with the link.
The fix
Add Open Graph tags plus a 1200×630 preview image to the homepage and each service page. One template change, about 1 hour.
💡 Every share becomes a mini-advert with your name, photo and 4.9★ rating attached.
🔍Google listingHigh impact

Google has no description of your homepage — it shows random text instead

The homepage has no meta description, and the title is only “Salwaty Home Healthcare LLC” (27 characters) — it never says home healthcare Dubai, home lab tests or doctor-at-home. When someone searches, Google pulls a random sentence as your snippet and has nothing to rank you for the exact service they typed.
The fix
Write a title and a 150-character description for the homepage and the main service pages (emergency consultation, lab tests at home, pediatric care). About 2 hours.
💡 More clicks from the rankings you already have — no extra ad spend.
🏷️Page structureMedium impact

Your homepage has zero H1 headings — the page never states what it does

The homepage contains 0 H1 headings and 112 H2 headings. The first visible headline “Safe, Compassionate, and Person-Centered Care” is an H2, so Google never gets one clear statement of your main service and city. That is the first thing its crawler looks for.
The fix
One H1: “Home Healthcare & Lab Tests at Home in Dubai”, then a tidy H2 structure for services. About 1 hour.
💡 Clear relevance for searches like &ldquo;home healthcare Dubai&rdquo; and &ldquo;lab test at home Dubai&rdquo;.
🗺️Local SEOMedium impact

You're invisible to Google's rich results — no structured data at all

There are zero ld+json schema blocks on the site. Without LocalBusiness / MedicalBusiness markup, Google cannot confidently show your rating, address, opening hours or service list in map packs and rich results — the boxes where most home-care searches actually end.
The fix
Add JSON-LD markup with your name, address, phone, 4.9 rating and services (one script block per page). About 1–2 hours.
💡 Eligible for star ratings and map-pack visibility — where the phone actually rings.
🐌SpeedMedium impact

The homepage is heavy — roughly 5 seconds on a mobile connection

The homepage weighs 573 KB and took 2.3–3.1 seconds just to respond in my test on a fast connection. On a Dubai 4G phone that usually means 5+ seconds. Mobile visitors start leaving after about 3. With 133 images on the page, most of that weight is pictures that are not compressed or lazy-loaded.
The fix
Compress and lazy-load images, enable caching. Same design, about half the weight. About half a day.
💡 Fewer families bouncing before they ever see your services or call button.
