🔍 The 5 things costing you patients
📱WhatsApp bookingHigh impact

There is no WhatsApp button anywhere on the site

The site only gives patients a phone number to call. In Hyderabad, the first thing a patient does is open WhatsApp — “can I get an appointment?”, a photo of the problem, a time that suits. With no WhatsApp button, every patient who prefers messaging simply books the next clinic.
The fix
Add a WhatsApp button on every page — fixed header, and a floating one on mobile — that opens a chat with a pre-filled booking message (“Hi, I'd like an appointment”). 30 minutes of work.
💡 Patients book the way they already talk. More enquiries, answered even after clinic hours.
🔍SEOHigh impact

The homepage has no H1 heading

I checked the page source: there are H2 headings but not a single H1. The H1 is the strongest on-page signal that tells Google what your page is about. Without it, Google has to guess — and guessing ranks you lower for searches like “dental hospital in Dilsukhnagar” or “dentist in Chaitanyapuri”.
The fix
Add one clear H1 — e.g. “Signature Smiles Dental Hospital — Trusted Dental Care in Chaitanyapuri, Hyderabad”.
💡 Better rankings for every local dental search around Chaitanyapuri and Dilsukhnagar.
👻Social linksMedium impact

Your Facebook, Instagram and YouTube icons lead nowhere

All six social icons on the site (header and footer) link to “#” — an empty link. A patient clicks the Instagram icon, and nothing happens. For a hospital with a 4.9 Google rating, those dead icons are hiding the proof you already have.
The fix
Point each icon to your actual profiles. If you don't have a profile yet, we can set them up.
💡 Patients see your real reviews and smiles gallery — trust goes up, and so does walk-in traffic.
🔀Booking flowMedium impact

“Book Appointment” sends patients off your website

The Book Appointment button opens an external booking page on a third-party domain (wcts.in). Patients are trained to distrust unexpected jumps to unknown websites — many close the tab and call the clinic next door instead.
The fix
Keep the booking on your own site (a simple form), or add a WhatsApp booking option right next to the button, so patients can book without leaving.
💡 Fewer patients dropping off between “want to book” and “booked”.
🖼️ImagesLow impact

3 of 7 images have no alt text

Alt text is how Google “sees” images — and how image search sends you traffic. Almost half your images are invisible to it.
The fix
Add descriptive alt text to each image (e.g. “Root canal treatment at Signature Smiles Dental Hospital”).
💡 Extra visits from Google Images, plus better accessibility for visually impaired patients.
